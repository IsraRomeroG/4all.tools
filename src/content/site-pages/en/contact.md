Email [hello@4all.tools](mailto:hello@4all.tools) to get in touch with
4all.tools.

## What you can contact us about

You can write to us with:

- feedback about the site or its tools;
- bug reports, including the page and steps needed to reproduce the problem;
- corrections to editorial content or translations;
- privacy or legal inquiries;
- general questions about 4all.tools.

Please include enough context for us to understand your message. Do not send
passwords, confidential files, or other sensitive information that is not
needed for your inquiry.

This page does not use a contact form. Your email is handled by your email
provider and the service that receives mail for 4all.tools.
